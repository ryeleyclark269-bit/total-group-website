About us

A crew you can actually reach

Total Group Contractors is a general and residential construction company built around a simple idea: one point of contact, a written schedule, and a crew that shows up when they say they will.

Our story

Straightforward construction, done properly

Total Group Contractors was founded to fix a common problem in residential construction: poor communication. We keep every project under a single dedicated contact, put the schedule in writing before work starts, and document every stage so there are no surprises at walkthrough.

Today our licensed crew handles new builds, additions, renovations and repairs for homeowners who want a contractor that answers the phone.

  • Fully licensed, bonded and insured general contractor
  • 13 years in business, 210+ completed projects
  • In-house crew across framing, electrical, plumbing and finishing
  • Written estimates and fixed project schedules
